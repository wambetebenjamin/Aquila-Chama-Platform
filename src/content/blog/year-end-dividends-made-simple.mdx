Dividend day is the best day of the chama year and, for the treasurer, the most
stressful. Here is the calculation, in the order that makes sense.

## Step one: total contributions

Add every contribution recorded as paid during the year. Exclude pending
entries. Exclude anything marked overdue, because that money has not arrived.

## Step two: add the interest the group earned

Interest is not the full repayment amount. If a member borrowed KES 100,000 at
ten per cent and is repaying KES 110,000, the interest is KES 10,000, not
KES 110,000. Getting this wrong is the single most common dividend error, and
it inflates the pot badly.

## Step three: subtract what is still out on loan

Money sitting with a borrower is not available to distribute. Subtract the total
outstanding loan balances.

That gives you the **distributable amount**.

> Total contributions + interest earned + other income − loans outstanding
> − agreed reserves = distributable amount

## Step four: choose the sharing rule

Groups generally use one of two rules:

- **Equal shares.** Every active member receives the same amount. Simple, and
  popular in welfare-oriented groups.
- **Proportional to contributions.** A member who contributed for twelve months
  receives more than one who joined in September. Fairer in investment groups.

Agree which rule you use at registration, not on dividend day.

## Step five: hold something back

Most groups retain a small reserve, often five to ten per cent, for the social
fund or for the first loan of the new year. Decide it by vote and record it.

## Step six: publish the number before the meeting

Send the calculation to every member on WhatsApp at least a day before the
meeting. Members who have time to read it arrive with questions instead of
suspicions.

## Worked example

| Line | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Contributions received | KES 700,000 |
| Interest earned on loans | KES 34,000 |
| Other income | KES 6,000 |
| Less loans outstanding | (KES 188,425) |
| Less reserve (5%) | (KES 27,579) |
| **Distributable** | **KES 524,000** |
| Active members | 12 |
| **Dividend per member** | **KES 43,667** |

Aquila Chama Platform produces this table automatically, from the same
transaction records that produced the monthly statements. There is one set of
numbers, and everybody can see them.
