Chamas do not lose money on bad loans. They lose money on slow loans. The
difference is almost always the guarantor system, and fixing it takes one
afternoon.

## Two guarantors, always

One guarantor creates a private arrangement between two people, which is exactly
what you do not want. Two guarantors create a group decision. On Aquila Chama
Platform both guarantors receive a WhatsApp notification the moment an
application is submitted, and both vote on the platform. A majority approves.

## Guarantors must have capacity

A guarantor with an outstanding loan of their own is not a guarantor. Before
approving, the group should be able to see at a glance whether each guarantor is
current on their contributions and what their existing loan balance is.

## The vote is the point

When a guarantor taps approve, they are on record. That single change moves
recovery from an awkward personal conversation to a shared responsibility that
was agreed in advance. It also protects the guarantor: they cannot be asked
later to cover a loan they never agreed to.

## Write the repayment date down

A loan without a written next-due date is a loan that will be renegotiated every
month. Set the date at disbursement, show the running balance, and let the
system flag the loan amber the day it goes overdue.

> The best time to agree what happens after a missed payment is before the
> money leaves the account.

## What to do when a payment is missed

1. **Day one:** an automatic WhatsApp reminder goes to the member. No human
   has to feel awkward.
2. **Day seven:** the treasurer calls. Most missed payments are a timing
   problem, not a willingness problem.
3. **Day thirty:** the guarantors are notified. This is the step groups avoid,
   and it is the step that works.
4. **Day sixty:** the group decides at a meeting, with minutes, whether to
   restructure or to recover from the guarantors.

## Transparency beats enforcement

Groups with a visible, shared loan register recover more money than groups with
stricter rules and no visibility. When every member can see the same balances,
social pressure does the enforcement and nobody has to be the villain.
